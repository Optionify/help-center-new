A Product reference option lets you offer **another product from your store** as a choice on a main product's page — a matching accessory, a warranty product, a care kit, or a bundled companion item. If you searched for a **Shopify product add-on option** or a **linked product option**, this guide shows how to set one up in a few minutes with [Optionify](https://apps.shopify.com/optionify), a free product options app.

## How do I offer another product as an add-on option in Shopify?

Shopify's native variants can only describe the current product — they can't reference other products in your catalog. With Optionify, create an option set, add a **Product reference** option, select the products from your store you want to offer, assign the set to your main products, and enable the theme app embed. Each linked product then appears as a selectable choice on the page.

## How the referenced product appears as a choice

When you add a **Product reference** option, you pick which products from your store to offer. Each one you select becomes a **choice** inside the option on the main product's page, tied to the real catalog item rather than a label you typed by hand. That keeps the add-on connected to an actual product you sell — its name stays in sync with your catalog, and the add-on is unambiguous for both the customer and your fulfillment team.

Because these choices live outside the variant system, they don't create SKUs or count against Shopify's [3-options-per-product limit](/articles/shopify-variant-limit), so you can offer several linked add-ons on a single product.

## How to set up a product reference option in Optionify

1. Click **Create option set** and name the set (e.g. "Recommended add-ons").
2. Add an option and choose the **Product reference** type.
3. Enter the option label, such as "Add a matching accessory" or "Protect your purchase."
4. Select the **products from your store** you want to offer as choices.
5. Give each choice an add-on price if it should charge extra (details below).
6. Decide whether the option is **required** — usually optional for cross-sells.
7. Assign the set with the right [assignment method](/articles/understanding-assign-types) — All Products, Tagged Products, Collections, or Individual Products.
8. Set the set to **Active** and save with Shopify's save bar.

![Product reference configuration in the Optionify Option Set Editor with selected products and add-on pricing](/images/product-reference-options/product-reference-editor.png)

Linked choices only appear on the storefront once the **theme app embed** is enabled in your theme editor, so switch it on before testing.

## Can a linked product add-on charge extra?

Yes. A Product reference choice can carry an **add-on price** in one of two modes:

- **Fixed** — a set amount, e.g. "Add care kit (+$19.00)."
- **Percent** — a percentage of the main product's price, e.g. "Add 2-year warranty (+10%)."

The add-on charge carries to checkout through a hidden companion product, so it works with **Buy it Now** and **Shop Pay** too. See [pricing variants](/articles/pricing-variants) for how the total reaches the order and [charging extra for an option](/articles/charge-extra-for-product-option) for more pricing patterns.

## Product reference vs. checkbox add-ons

Both offer extras, but they're built differently:

- **[Checkbox add-ons](/articles/checkbox-options)** — you type each extra as a value with its own price. Best for simple options like "Gift wrap" or "Extra battery" that aren't full catalog products.
- **Product reference** — you link to an actual product in your store as the choice. Best when the add-on is a **genuine product you sell** and want to keep connected to your catalog.

If the add-on is a real product with its own page and details, use a product reference. If it's a small extra that doesn't warrant its own product, a checkbox is simpler.

## Real use cases for linked product add-ons

**Matching accessories.** A phone product offers a case and a charger — both real products in your store — as selectable add-ons on the product page.

**Warranty and protection products.** A store links a warranty product as a choice, letting customers add coverage without leaving the page.

**Care kits and consumables.** A furniture or footwear store offers a cleaning or care kit as a linked add-on to the main item.

**Curated bundles.** A gift or beauty shop links complementary products as choices, building a soft bundle without creating a separate bundle SKU.

For broader cross-selling patterns and how add-on charges flow to the order, see [Shopify product add-ons on the product page](/articles/shopify-product-add-ons). Because every choice is saved as a line item property on the order, your fulfillment team sees exactly which linked products the customer added.
