## What does "free" really mean for a product options app?

A truly free product options app charges nothing to use its core features at production scale — not a capped trial, not a plan you outgrow in a month. By that definition most popular apps are *freemium*: a limited free tier exists, but conditional logic, extra option types, or higher usage require a paid monthly plan. Only a few apps give every feature away with no upgrade path.

This matters because product options are not a nice-to-have you can scale back. Once your storefront depends on an engraving field or a gift-wrap add-on, hitting a paywall mid-growth is disruptive. So the real question is not "does it have a free plan?" but "will I still be free when my store grows?"

Both the free and paid apps here solve the same underlying Shopify limit: products are still capped at **3 options** even after the variant ceiling rose to 2,048 in October 2025. See the [Shopify variant limit guide](/articles/shopify-variant-limit) for why that 3-option cap is the real bottleneck.
