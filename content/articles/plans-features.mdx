**How much does Optionify cost? Optionify is free — one plan, $0 per month, every feature included.** There are no Starter, Professional, or Enterprise tiers and no transaction fees. This page explains exactly what "free" means so you can plan with confidence.

## Does Optionify have multiple plans?

No. Optionify has a **single plan that is free forever**. Unlike apps that split features across pricing tiers, Optionify gives every merchant the complete product — all 17 option types, unlimited option sets, unlimited products, conditional logic, and add-on pricing — from the moment you install. For the full story on why it's free, see [Optionify: Free Forever](/articles/free-forever).

## What's included for free

Everything. The single free plan includes:

- **All 17 option types** — text, textarea, dropdown, select box, radio buttons, checkbox, switch, number, email, phone, date picker, file/image upload, color dropdown, image swatch, range slider, dimension, and product reference
- **Unlimited option sets** and **unlimited products** — no caps
- **4 assignment methods** — All Products, Tagged Products, Collections, and Individual Products ([details](/articles/understanding-assign-types))
- **Add-on pricing** — fixed or percentage charges on any option or value ([details](/articles/charge-extra-for-product-option))
- **Conditional logic** — show or hide options based on selections ([details](/articles/conditional-logic))
- **Order integration** — every selection is saved to the Shopify order for fulfillment
- **Mobile-responsive** rendering that works with standard Shopify themes

## Are there any transaction fees?

No. Optionify takes no cut of your sales and adds no per-order fees. Any add-on price a customer pays flows to you through your normal Shopify checkout, including **Buy it Now** and **Shop Pay**. The only costs on your store are Shopify's own platform fees, which are unrelated to Optionify.

## Are there any limits?

There are no artificial limits inside Optionify — use as many option sets, options, values, and products as you need. The only ceilings you'll encounter are Shopify's own platform constraints, such as the [3-options-per-product variant limit](/articles/shopify-variant-limit) that Optionify is specifically designed to help you work around.

## How do I get started?

1. **[Install Optionify](https://apps.shopify.com/optionify)** from the Shopify App Store — no payment or credit card required.
2. Enable the theme app embed so options render on your storefront.
3. Follow [Creating Your First Product Option](/articles/creating-your-first-product-option) to build your first option set.
