Charging extra for a premium choice — engraving, gift wrap, rush service, a warranty — is one of the most requested things Shopify merchants can't do with native variants. **Shopify product option pricing** in [Optionify](https://apps.shopify.com/optionify) fixes that: attach a fixed or percentage **add-on price** to any option or value, and the charge follows the customer all the way to the order. This guide explains both pricing modes and exactly how the money reaches checkout.

## How does Shopify product option pricing work?

Each option or value can carry an add-on price in one of two modes: **Fixed** (a set money amount) or **Percent** (a percentage of the product price). When a customer selects a priced option, the extra charge is added on top of the product price and carried through to checkout and the order. There is no separate checkout step — the customer pays one total.

## Fixed vs. percentage add-on pricing

Both modes are live; choose based on how the charge should behave:

- **Fixed** — a flat money amount, the same for every product the option is assigned to. Best for concrete services with a set cost: "Engraving (+$10.00)," "Gift wrap (+$5.00)," "Assembly (+$40.00)."
- **Percent** — a percentage of the product's price, so the charge scales with the item. Best for value-based add-ons: "Extended warranty (+15%)" costs more on a $2,000 sofa than on a $200 chair, automatically.

You can mix both across a single option set — a fixed charge on one value and a percentage on another — so each add-on is priced the way that makes sense for it. For a focused walkthrough of a single upsell charge, see [charging extra for a product option](/articles/charge-extra-for-product-option).

## How add-on charges reach checkout

This is the part most merchants worry about, so here is exactly what happens. When a customer selects a priced option, Optionify applies the add-on amount through a **hidden companion product**. That companion carries the extra charge and is added alongside the main item, so the customer's total reflects every option they chose.

Because the charge rides on a real product behind the scenes rather than a script that only runs in the cart, it survives the fast checkout paths:

- **Buy it Now** — the add-on is included even when the customer skips the cart entirely.
- **Shop Pay** express checkout — the extra amount travels with the order.
- **Standard cart and checkout** — the charge shows in the running total as expected.

The customer never sees a "companion product"; they see one clean line item with the price they expect. Multiple selected options simply **add together** into the total.

## Where add-on charges appear in the order

Every option a customer selects is saved as a **line item property** on the Shopify order, and the add-on amount is included in the order total through the companion product. When you open the order in Shopify admin, you see which paid options were chosen — the engraving text, the wrap style, the selected warranty — and the charge applied for each.

This means your fulfillment team works from the order itself: no separate spreadsheet, no guessing which extras the customer paid for. Option data flowing straight into orders is core to how Optionify [adds options without variants](/articles/add-options-without-variants).

## Option pricing vs. Shopify variants

Optionify pricing complements variants rather than replacing them. The rule of thumb:

| Use Shopify variants for | Use Optionify priced options for |
|--------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Size, Color, Material | Engraving, personalization |
| Stock-tracked SKUs | Gift wrap, rush, assembly |
| Inventory combinations | Warranties and services |
| The 3 core product options | Everything beyond the 3-option cap |

Priced options are **not variants**, so they never create SKUs or count against Shopify's 3-options-per-product limit — the cap that stayed in place even after Shopify raised the variant ceiling to 2,048 in October 2025 (see the [variant limit guide](/articles/shopify-variant-limit)). Keep variants for stock-tracked combinations like "Blue / XL," and hand every priced add-on to Optionify.

## Which option types support add-on pricing?

Add-on pricing works across choice and toggle types, so you can price the format that fits the sale:

- **[Switch](/articles/switch-options)** — charge when toggled on (gift wrap, rush).
- **[Checkbox](/articles/checkbox-options)** — price each stackable extra; selected boxes add up.
- **[Radio](/articles/radio-options)** and **[Dropdown](/articles/dropdown-options)** — price individual choices in a single-select menu.
- **Dimension** — area-based pricing (unit price × width × height) for made-to-measure items.
- **Color and image swatches** — charge more for premium finishes or materials.

Pair pricing with [conditional logic](/articles/conditional-logic) to reveal paid options only when relevant, keeping the page clean while still capturing the upsell.

## Setting up an add-on price step by step

1. Open or create your set with **Create option set**.
2. Add an option and enter its values (for example, a Switch "Add gift wrap?").
3. On the option or value, enter an **add-on price** and choose **Fixed** or **Percent**.
4. Assign the set with the right [assignment method](/articles/understanding-assign-types).
5. Set the set to **Active** and save with Shopify's save bar.
6. Enable the **theme app embed** in your theme editor so options render on the storefront.
7. Use the editor's **Preview** action to open an assigned product and confirm the charge appears.

## FAQ

**How does Shopify product option pricing work in Optionify?**
Each option or value carries an add-on price in Fixed or Percent mode. When a customer selects it, the charge is added on top of the product price and carried through to checkout and the order.

**What's the difference between fixed and percentage add-on pricing?**
Fixed adds a flat amount, like +$10.00. Percent adds a share of the product price, like +15%, so it scales with the item's cost. Both are live and reach checkout the same way.

**Do add-on charges work with Buy it Now and Shop Pay?**
Yes. Charges apply through a hidden companion product, so the extra amount is included even when a customer uses Buy it Now or Shop Pay express checkout.

**Where do add-on charges appear in the Shopify order?**
Selections appear as line item properties on the order, and the add-on amount is included in the total. Fulfillment sees which paid options were chosen and the charge for each.

**Does option pricing create Shopify variants?**
No. Priced options aren't variants, so they never create SKUs or count against the 3-options-per-product limit.
