If you are searching for a **Globo Product Options alternative**, you have probably already used Globo and hit a wall — a paid tier, a missing option type, or clunky conditional logic. This is an honest comparison between Globo and [Optionify](https://apps.shopify.com/optionify), a **fully free** Shopify product options app. We will be fair about where Globo wins, then show exactly where Optionify pulls ahead.

## Is Globo Product Options a good app?

Yes. Globo is one of the most established product options apps on the Shopify App Store, with roughly 4,600 reviews and a 4.9-star rating. That kind of track record is earned. It offers a wide range of option types, works across most themes, and has years of stability behind it. If an app's maturity and review count are your top priority, Globo is a legitimate, safe choice — we are not going to pretend otherwise.

Where Globo frustrates merchants is usually cost and complexity. Its most useful features — beyond the limited free tier — sit on paid monthly plans, and its conditional logic is configured through forms rather than a visual view. That is exactly the gap a **Globo Product Options alternative** should close.

## Why look for a Globo alternative?

Merchants typically start hunting for an alternative for three reasons: they do not want a recurring monthly bill for something Shopify almost does natively, they want conditional logic that is easier to reason about, or they need a specific pricing model like charging by dimensions. Optionify was built around all three.

The core problem both apps solve is the same: Shopify still caps every product at **3 options**, even after raising the variant ceiling to 2,048 in October 2025. See our [Shopify variant limit guide](/articles/shopify-variant-limit) for why the 3-option cap — not the variant count — is the real bottleneck.

## What makes Optionify different?

Optionify's three real differentiators are worth spelling out, because generic "we have more features" claims are meaningless.

**It is genuinely free.** Not a free trial, not a limited tier that nudges you to upgrade. Every merchant gets all 17 option types, all four assignment methods, conditional logic, and add-on pricing at no cost, with no transaction fees on the options you create. Read [why Optionify is free forever](/articles/free-forever) for the full picture.

**Conditional logic is visual.** Instead of filling in "if this then that" form rows, you build show/hide rules on a [visual flow canvas](/articles/conditional-logic). When a "Add engraving?" switch reveals a text field that itself reveals a font dropdown, you can see the whole branch at a glance. This is the single most common reason merchants tell us they moved off a form-based competitor.

**Dimension and area pricing is built in.** The **Dimension** option type takes width × height inputs and multiplies by a unit price, so blinds, banners, framing, fabric, and glass shops can quote by area automatically. That is awkward or impossible on many competitors.

## How to switch from Globo to Optionify

Switching is low-risk because neither app modifies your actual Shopify products or variants — they only add an options layer through a theme app embed.

1. **[Install Optionify free](https://apps.shopify.com/optionify)** from the Shopify App Store.
2. Rebuild your option groups as [option sets](/articles/creating-your-first-product-option). Most merchants find this faster than expected since one set can cover a whole collection or tag — see [understanding assign types](/articles/understanding-assign-types).
3. Recreate any add-on prices with [pricing and variants](/articles/pricing-variants), choosing fixed or percent charges.
4. Enable the Optionify theme app embed in your theme editor, then **preview** an assigned product to confirm rendering.
5. Once everything looks right, disable Globo's app embed so options do not render twice.

## Who should switch — and who should not

**Switch to Optionify if** you want to stop paying monthly for product options, you rely on conditional logic and want it easier to manage, or you sell anything priced by size or area. Free plus a visual logic canvas plus dimension pricing is a combination Globo does not match without a paid plan.

**Stay on Globo if** you have a large, working configuration you do not want to rebuild, and you place a high value on the reassurance of ~4,600 reviews and years of production use. There is nothing wrong with Globo — it is simply not free, and its logic editor is form-based.
