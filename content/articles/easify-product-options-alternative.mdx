If you are comparing an **Easify Product Options alternative**, you are looking at one of the strongest apps in the category — so the bar is high. This is an honest comparison between Easify and [Optionify](https://apps.shopify.com/optionify), a **fully free** Shopify product options app. We will give Easify credit where it is due, then show where a free alternative with a visual logic canvas makes more sense.

## Is Easify Product Options a good app?

Yes. Easify has risen quickly to roughly 2,800 reviews and carries the **Built for Shopify** badge, which Shopify only grants apps that meet strict quality, performance, and UX standards. That badge is meaningful — it signals a fast, well-built app that plays nicely with the admin (Optionify carries the same badge). Easify supports a broad set of option types and has a clean, modern interface. If you want a polished app with a large review base and price is not your main concern, Easify is a genuinely good pick.

Where merchants start looking elsewhere is pricing structure and conditional logic. Easify's free tier is limited, so growing stores eventually move onto paid plans, and its logic rules are configured through forms. That is where a **Easify Product Options alternative** like Optionify changes the equation.

## Why consider an Easify alternative?

Both apps exist to solve the same core Shopify constraint: every product is still capped at **3 options**, even after the variant limit rose to 2,048 in October 2025. The 3-option cap — not the variant count — is what blocks personalization, add-ons, and made-to-order products. Our [Shopify variant limit guide](/articles/shopify-variant-limit) explains this in full.

Given both apps clear that hurdle, the decision comes down to three things: whether you want to pay monthly, how you prefer to build conditional logic, and whether you need pricing by dimensions. Optionify was designed to win on all three.

## What makes Optionify different?

**It is genuinely free.** Every merchant gets all 17 option types, all four assignment methods, conditional logic, and add-on pricing with no paid tier and no transaction fees on the options you create. There is nothing to upgrade into as your store grows. See [why Optionify is free forever](/articles/free-forever).

**Conditional logic is visual.** Rather than filling in form rows, you build show/hide rules on a [visual flow canvas](/articles/conditional-logic). When one selection reveals another option, and that reveals a third, you can see the entire branch laid out. Merchants who manage complex configurators consistently prefer seeing the flow to reading a list of rules.

**Dimension and area pricing is built in.** The **Dimension** option type takes width × height and multiplies by a unit price, so stores selling blinds, signage, framing, glass, or fabric can quote by area automatically — a scenario that is awkward on most competitors.

Beyond those three, Optionify covers the essentials you would expect: [text fields](/articles/text-options), [dropdowns](/articles/dropdown-options), [date pickers](/articles/datepicker-options) with blocked weekdays, and [file uploads](/articles/image-options), all writing straight to the Shopify order as line item properties.

## How to switch from Easify to Optionify

Switching is low-risk. Neither app changes your Shopify products or variants — they only add an options layer via a theme app embed.

1. **[Install Optionify free](https://apps.shopify.com/optionify)** from the Shopify App Store.
2. Rebuild your groups as [option sets](/articles/creating-your-first-product-option) and assign them using [assign types](/articles/understanding-assign-types) — one set can cover a whole tag or collection.
3. Recreate add-on prices with [pricing and variants](/articles/pricing-variants), choosing fixed or percent.
4. Enable Optionify's theme app embed, then **preview** an assigned product to confirm it renders correctly.
5. Disable Easify's app embed so options do not display twice.

## Who should switch — and who should not

**Switch to Optionify if** you would rather not pay monthly for product options, you want conditional logic you can see rather than read, or you sell anything priced by size or area. Free plus a visual logic canvas plus dimension pricing is hard to beat.

**Stay on Easify if** you already have a large working setup you would rather not rebuild and monthly cost is not a concern. Easify is a well-made app, and like Optionify it carries the Built for Shopify badge — so the trade-off is not quality, it is simply price and a form-based logic editor.
