If you are weighing **Globo vs Easify product options**, you are comparing two of the strongest apps in the category — which is exactly why the decision is hard. This is an honest, three-way look: Globo versus Easify head to head, then where [Optionify](https://apps.shopify.com/optionify), a **fully free** Shopify product options app, fits as the challenger. Both leaders are good; the real question is what you are paying for and how much conditional-logic and pricing flexibility you need.

## Globo vs Easify: what is the real difference?

Globo and Easify solve the same problem — adding custom options and add-ons beyond Shopify's hard cap of **3 options per product** — but they come at it differently. Globo is the older, more established app with roughly 4,600 reviews at 4.9 stars, deep theme compatibility, and years of production stability. Easify is the newer challenger, with around 2,800 reviews, the **Built for Shopify** badge, and a cleaner, more modern admin UI. Both tier their pricing.

The honest summary: Globo wins on maturity and review volume; Easify wins on interface polish and its Shopify-verified badge. Neither is free once you grow.

## Is Globo Product Options a good app?

Yes. Globo is one of the most established options apps on the Shopify App Store. Its ~4,600 reviews and 4.9-star rating are earned — it supports a wide range of option types, works across most themes, and has a long track record. Its documentation is thorough (it is docs-only, with no marketing blog). If app maturity and a large review base are your top priority, Globo is a safe, legitimate choice.

Where Globo frustrates merchants is cost and conditional logic. Its most useful features sit on paid monthly plans beyond a limited free tier, and its show/hide rules are configured through forms rather than a visual view.

## Is Easify Product Options a good app?

Also yes. Easify has climbed quickly to ~2,800 reviews and carries the **Built for Shopify** badge, which Shopify grants only to apps meeting strict quality, performance, and UX standards. Its interface is modern and clean, and it supports a broad set of option types. If you want a polished, Shopify-verified app and price is not your main concern, Easify is a strong pick.

The same two caveats apply: its free tier is limited, so growing stores move onto paid plans, and its logic rules are form-based.

## Which should you choose?

Choose **Globo** if you want the largest review base and years of proven stability, and you are comfortable paying for advanced features. Choose **Easify** if you value a Built-for-Shopify badge and a polished, modern interface, and price is secondary. Choose **Optionify** if cost matters, you want conditional logic that is easier to reason about, or you sell anything priced by size or area — free, plus a visual logic canvas, plus dimension pricing is a combination neither leader matches without a paid plan.
