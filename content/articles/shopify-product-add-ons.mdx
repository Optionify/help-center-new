Accessories, warranties, gift wrap, priority handling — the extras a customer taps "yes" to on the way to checkout are some of the easiest revenue you'll ever add. This guide shows how to offer **Shopify product add-ons on the product page** with real prices, without variants and without touching your theme code.

## What counts as a product add-on?

A product add-on is an **optional, usually chargeable extra** a customer can select on a product page, on top of the main item — a warranty for a laptop, a memory card for a camera, gift wrap for a present. It's the customer's choice, and it adds to the line price.

Before you build, scope it correctly: an add-on is **not** a bundle. A bundle is a fixed set of products sold together as a single package at one price. An add-on is optional and per-order. If you want optional extras on a product page, that's an add-on — and that's what Optionify does. Bundles are a different tool.

## Why Shopify variants can't do add-ons

Shopify variants describe fixed versions of one product — Blue / Large, 128 GB / Space Grey. They're built for inventory combinations, not optional extras. Trying to model "add a $99 warranty?" as a variant doubles your variant count, needs a SKU, and even then it can't offer a **different product** as the add-on. And every product is still capped at [3 options](/articles/shopify-variant-limit), so warranty + gift wrap + engraving simply won't fit.

Add-ons aren't inventory combinations — they're extra choices. That's exactly what a product options app is for.

## How to add product add-ons on the product page

[Optionify](https://apps.shopify.com/optionify) adds chargeable add-ons as custom options that render on the product page and never become variants. It's free, and there are two ways to build them.

### 1. Simple chargeable add-ons (checkbox or switch)

For extras that are just "yes, add it," use a **Switch** (a single on/off toggle) or a **Checkbox** (a list where the customer can tick several). Give each one an [add-on price](/articles/charge-extra-for-product-option) — a fixed amount or a percentage of the product price — and it's charged when selected.

```
☑ Add 2-year warranty        +$49.00
☑ Gift wrap this item        +$6.00
☑ Priority handling          +$9.00
```

The [Switch type](/articles/switch-options) suits a single yes/no add-on; the [Checkbox type](/articles/checkbox-options) suits a menu of optional extras a customer can combine. Both update the price live on the page.

![Optionify add-ons on a product page: a switch for warranty and a checkbox list of accessories with prices](/images/shopify-product-add-ons/product-page-addons.png)
*Chargeable add-ons render directly on the product page, each with its own price.*

### 2. Offer a real product as an add-on (Product reference)

Sometimes the add-on is an actual product you stock — a case, a memory card, a spare filter. Optionify's **Product reference** option type lets you **link a real product from your store** as an add-on choice. The customer adds it straight from the main product page, and it's carried through as a companion item at checkout.

```
Buying: Mirrorless Camera
  ↳ Add-on (Product reference): 64 GB SD Card
  ↳ Add-on (Product reference): Padded Camera Case
```

This is the honest way to upsell a genuine SKU without redirecting the customer to another page.

## How do add-ons behave in the cart and checkout?

This is where a lot of options apps get sloppy, so it's worth being precise. Optionify sends the add-on charge to checkout through a **hidden companion product**. The practical results:

- The add-on price is **collected correctly at checkout**, not just shown on the page.
- It works with the standard cart **and** with **Buy it Now / Shop Pay** express checkout.
- The customer's selections are saved on the Shopify order as **line item properties**, so fulfillment sees exactly which add-ons were chosen.

Because the data lands on the order, your team knows to include the warranty registration, the gift wrap, or the extra product when they pack it.

| Use case | Best Optionify type |

| Warranty / protection plan | Switch or Checkbox (priced) |
| Accessories from your catalog | Product reference |

Pair add-ons with [conditional logic](/articles/conditional-logic) to keep them relevant — for example, only show "add installation" when the customer picks a "professional install" delivery choice.

## How to set it up

1. **[Install Optionify](https://apps.shopify.com/optionify)** — free, all option types included.
2. Click **Create option set** and open the Option Set Editor.
3. Add a **Switch** or **Checkbox** for simple extras, or a **Product reference** to link an existing product. Set each add-on's price (fixed or percent).
4. [Assign the set](/articles/understanding-assign-types) to the right products — a single item, a collection, tagged products, or all products.
5. Set the set to **Active**, save via the save bar, and use **Preview** to check it on the storefront.

## FAQ

### How do I add product add-ons on the Shopify product page?

Install Optionify, create an option set, add a **checkbox** or **switch** with an add-on price (or a **Product reference** to link a real product), then assign it to the relevant products. The add-on renders on the product page and its charge carries through to checkout.

### What's the difference between an add-on and a bundle?

An add-on is an optional extra a customer ticks on the product page — a warranty, gift wrap, an accessory — priced on top of the main item. A bundle is a fixed group of products sold together as one package. Optionify handles add-ons.

### Do add-ons create variants?

No. Optionify add-ons are custom options, not variants, so they don't count against the 3-option limit, don't need SKUs, and don't inflate your variant count. Add as many as a product needs.
