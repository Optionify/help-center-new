If you are looking for an **Advanced Product Options alternative** — specifically to Mageworx APO — you have probably seen its headline claim of "31 option types" and wondered whether you need all of them. This is an honest comparison between Mageworx Advanced Product Options and [Optionify](https://apps.shopify.com/optionify), a **fully free** Shopify product options app. We will give APO credit where it is due, then counter that 31-types trust signal honestly: more option types is not the same as more value.

## Is Mageworx Advanced Product Options a good app?

Yes. Mageworx APO is a mature, capable app from a long-established developer with roots in the broader e-commerce tooling world. Its marketing centers on breadth — it advertises **31 option types** — and it supports conditional logic, add-on pricing, and inventory-aware options. For merchants who want the widest possible menu of input types on paper and are comfortable on a paid plan, APO is a legitimate, feature-rich choice.

Where merchants start looking elsewhere is the pricing model and the honest reality behind the type count. APO is a paid app, and its feature set sits on monthly plans. And that headline "31 option types" deserves a closer look.

## Does Optionify have as many option types as APO?

No — Optionify ships **17 option types** to APO's advertised 31. But that gap is smaller than it sounds. A high type count usually reflects splitting near-identical inputs into separate entries — several flavors of text field, several styles of the same dropdown, minor swatch variations. What matters is whether the types you will actually use are present and free.

Optionify's 17 cover every practical customization: text input, textarea, dropdown, select box, radio buttons, checkbox, switch, number, email, phone, date picker, file/image upload, color dropdown, image swatch, range slider, dimension with area pricing, and product reference. If a type you need is on that list — and for most stores, all of them are — the number above 17 is marketing, not capability.

## Why "31 option types" is not the whole story

The 31-types number is a strong trust signal, and it is worth countering honestly rather than dismissing. Three points matter:

**Overlap inflates the count.** Many option-type lists split what is functionally one input into several entries. A merchant rarely needs six ways to render a dropdown; they need one that works. Optionify's 17 avoid that padding.

**Free beats paid breadth.** Even if APO's extra types were all distinct, they sit behind a monthly bill. Optionify gives you all 17 types plus conditional logic and add-on pricing at no cost — see our [best free Shopify product options app](/articles/best-free-shopify-product-options-app) roundup for context.

**Logic and pricing flexibility matter more than count.** Where Optionify genuinely differentiates is the [visual conditional logic canvas](/articles/conditional-logic) — you build show/hide rules and see how selections branch, rather than filling in form rows like APO — and the **Dimension** type, which multiplies width × height by a unit price for blinds, banners, framing, and glass. Those capabilities move revenue more than a longer type list.

The core problem both apps solve is the same: Shopify still caps every product at **3 options**, even after raising the variant ceiling to 2,048 in October 2025. See our [Shopify variant limit guide](/articles/shopify-variant-limit) for why the 3-option cap — not the variant count — is the real bottleneck.

## How to switch from Mageworx APO to Optionify

Switching is low-risk because neither app modifies your actual Shopify products or variants — they only add an options layer through a theme app embed.

1. **[Install Optionify free](https://apps.shopify.com/optionify)** from the Shopify App Store.
2. Rebuild your option groups as [option sets](/articles/creating-your-first-product-option). Map each APO option to one of Optionify's 17 types — you will find every practical input has a home.
3. Recreate any add-on prices with [pricing and variants](/articles/pricing-variants), choosing fixed or percent charges.
4. Enable the Optionify theme app embed in your theme editor, then **preview** an assigned product to confirm rendering.
5. Once everything looks right, disable APO's app embed so options do not render twice.

## Who should switch — and who should not

**Switch to Optionify if** you want to stop paying monthly for product options, you value conditional logic that is easier to manage, or you sell anything priced by size or area. Free plus a visual logic canvas plus dimension pricing beats a longer type list for most stores.

**Stay on Mageworx APO if** you genuinely rely on a specific option type that maps to none of Optionify's 17, or you are deeply invested in APO's ecosystem and the paid cost is not a concern. There is nothing wrong with APO — it is simply paid, its logic editor is form-based, and its headline type count includes more overlap than it appears.
