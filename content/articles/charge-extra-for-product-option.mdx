You added a gift-wrap toggle or a "premium material" choice to your product page — but the price never changes at checkout. That's because native Shopify custom fields don't carry a price. Here's how to **charge extra for a product option on Shopify**, using fixed or percentage add-on pricing that reaches checkout correctly.

## Why native custom fields can't charge extra

Shopify's built-in way to collect option input is **line-item properties** — custom fields you add to the product form in your theme. They're great at capturing text like "Engraving: Sofia," but they have one hard limitation: **they add no price.**

A line-item property is just a label and a value attached to the order. There's no field for money, so however you build it — gift wrap, rush service, premium fabric — the customer pays the base price and nothing more. To actually charge for an option, the extra amount has to become a real line on the order that checkout can total. That's exactly what an app handles.

## The fix: Optionify add-on pricing

[Optionify](https://apps.shopify.com/optionify) lets you attach an **add-on price** to any option or value. When a customer selects it, the extra amount is added to their total at checkout. You set this per option in the **Option Set Editor**, and there are two modes — both live:

- **Fixed** — a set money amount (e.g. +$5.00 for gift wrap)
- **Percent** — a percentage of the product's price (e.g. +15% for a premium material)

![Optionify Option Set Editor showing the add-on price field with Fixed and Percent modes](/images/charge-extra-for-product-option/add-on-pricing.png)

### Fixed vs Percent — which to use

Pick the mode that matches how the upgrade should scale:

- **Fixed** is best for flat add-ons that cost the same regardless of the product: gift wrapping, rush processing, a stamped card. +$5 is +$5 whether the item is $20 or $200.
- **Percent** is best for upgrades that should scale with the base price: a premium material on a $40 shirt versus a $400 jacket shouldn't cost the same flat fee. A percentage keeps the surcharge proportional.

## How the extra charge reaches checkout

This is where a lot of options apps quietly fail. Optionify sends add-on charges to checkout through a **hidden companion product** — the extra amount rides along as a separate, invisible line so checkout totals it correctly.

The important consequence: it **works with Buy it Now and Shop Pay**, not just the standard cart. Express and accelerated checkouts skip the cart page, so approaches that only adjust the cart lose the surcharge. The companion-product method means the extra charge is collected wherever the customer checks out.

Every selection and its price also appear on the Shopify order as line-item properties, so your fulfillment team sees exactly what was chosen and what was charged.

## How to charge extra for an option, step by step

1. **[Install Optionify](https://apps.shopify.com/optionify)** — free forever, add-on pricing included
2. Enable the Optionify **app embed** in your theme editor so options render on the storefront
3. Click **Create option set** (or open an existing set in the **Option Set Editor**)
4. Add the option you want to charge for — for example a **Switch** for gift wrap, or a **Radio buttons** option for material
5. On the option or value, set the **add-on price** and choose **Fixed** or **Percent**
6. Choose an **assignment**: all products, by tag, by collection, or individual products ([assignment types](/articles/understanding-assign-types))
7. Set the set to **Active** and save via Shopify's save bar
8. Use **Preview** to open an assigned product and confirm the extra charge appears in the total

## Use cases: what to charge extra for

Add-on pricing fits any option that adds cost or value:

- **Gift wrap** — a [Switch option](/articles/switch-options) that toggles on a flat +$5, perfect for Fixed pricing
- **Rush / priority processing** — a fixed fee for faster turnaround
- **Premium material or finish** — a Percent add-on that scales with the product price
- **Engraving or embroidery** — a fixed fee on top of a text input
- **Add-on products or accessories** — a fixed charge for a bundled extra
- **Size or dimension upgrades** — pair with the Dimension type for area-based pricing

For a deeper look at pricing options and how add-on charges behave in the cart and order, see [pricing product options](/articles/pricing-variants).

## Does charging extra cost anything?

No. Optionify is **free forever**, and that includes add-on pricing in both Fixed and Percent modes. There are no paid tiers, no premium plan, and no transaction fees on the extra amounts you collect. You keep what you charge.

## FAQ

### How do I charge extra for a product option on Shopify?

Native line-item properties add no price. Use Optionify: add an add-on price to any option — a fixed amount or a percentage of the product price — and it's collected at checkout, including on Buy it Now and Shop Pay.

### Can I charge a percentage instead of a fixed amount?

Yes. Optionify supports both. Fixed adds a set money amount; Percent adds a percentage of the product's price, useful for premium upgrades that should scale with the base price. Both modes are live.

### Does the extra charge work with Shop Pay?

Yes. Optionify sends add-on charges to checkout through a hidden companion product, so the extra amount is collected correctly on express checkouts like Buy it Now and Shop Pay, not just the standard cart.
