Most guides still warn about the "100 variant limit." In 2026 that number is actually **2,048** — but the limit that really blocks you hasn't changed: **3 options per product**. Here's how to add unlimited options anyway.

## Shopify's limits in 2026

- **Variants per product: up to 2,048** — raised from 100 on October 15, 2025 (all plans, free)
- **Options per product: still 3** — e.g. Size, Color, Material. *Unchanged.*
- Every variant still needs inventory tracking and a unique SKU

The variant increase grabbed headlines, but the **3-option cap — not the variant count — is the wall most stores hit first.** Want Size, Color, Material *and* a custom text field? There's no room.

## The fix: add options without variants

[Optionify](https://apps.shopify.com/optionify) adds **unlimited product options** as custom fields that never count as variants. Use Shopify variants for inventory, and Optionify for everything else:

```
Size, Color, Material   → Shopify variants (inventory)
Custom text, font,      → Optionify options (no variant,
gift wrap, upload,          no SKU, no limit)
delivery date …
```

What you get:

- **17 option types** — text, textarea, dropdown, radio, checkbox, switch, number, email, phone, date picker, file upload, color & image swatches, range slider, dimension, and more
- **4 assignment methods** — all products, by tag, by collection, or individual products ([details](/articles/understanding-assign-types))
- **Conditional logic** — show options only when relevant ([details](/articles/conditional-logic))
- **Add-on pricing** — charge extra for premium options ([details](/articles/pricing-variants))

Options appear on the product page and their data flows straight into the order.

## Why variants aren't the answer

Most customizations aren't inventory, so they don't belong in variants at all:

| Option | Needs a variant? |
|--------|------------------|
| Engraving / custom text | No — personalization |
| Gift message & wrapping | No — customer input |
| File / logo upload | No — unique per order |
| Delivery date | No — a preference |
| Add-on service (rush, etc.) | No — priced without stock |

Keep variants for what they're built for (stock-tracked combinations like Blue / XL) and hand everything else to Optionify.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the Shopify variant limit in 2026?

Up to **2,048 variants per product** (raised from 100 on October 15, 2025) — but still a maximum of **3 options** per product. The 3-option limit hasn't changed.

### How do I add more than 3 options?

Use Optionify. It adds unlimited options as custom fields, so you're no longer capped at 3 — and they don't require variants or SKUs.

### Will option data appear in my orders?

Yes. Every selection appears in the Shopify order, so fulfillment is straightforward.
