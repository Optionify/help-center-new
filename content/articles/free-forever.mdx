## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the free plan a trial?

No. The Free plan doesn't expire — it's 2 option sets with the simpler input types, for as long as you want to use it. No credit card required to install.

### What's the difference between Starter and Pro?

Starter unlocks unlimited option sets and every option type (swatches, file upload, date picker, dimension, product reference, and more), plus required options and custom CSS/JS. Pro adds the features that charge customers extra or automate your workflow: add-on pricing, conditional logic, CSV import/export, and advanced validation.

### Can I try Starter or Pro before paying?

Yes, both come with a 7-day free trial.

### What if I need support?

Support is included on every plan, starting with email and chat on Free.

### Are there transaction fees?

No. Whatever plan you're on, Optionify never takes a cut of your sales.
